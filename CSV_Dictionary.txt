CSV AND JSON FIELD GUIDE

All summaries cover the supplied 2024-01-01 to 2024-12-30 snapshot.
attempts: all payment records in the group.
successful / unsuccessful: record counts after status normalization.
attempted_cents / successful_cents / unsuccessful_cents: integer hundredths
of the assumed INR unit. Divide by 100 for INR, or 1,000,000,000 for INR crore.
success_rate: decimal proportion, e.g. 0.96 = 96%.
active_users: distinct User_ID values with an attempt in the group.
pre_join: attempts before the profile's Join_Date.
status_issues: raw status is neither Successful nor Failed.
days: number of covered calendar dates in the month or weekday grouping.
attempts_per_day: attempts / covered calendar dates.
services (top_users.csv): distinct services used by that user.
AgeBand: profile age groups 18–24, 25–34, 35–44, 45–54 and 55+.

User counts cannot be added across overlapping months, services or weekdays.
The first row of each CSV gives the grouping and metric column names.
Top users are sorted by attempted value, not name and not successful value.
Original source service values retain underscores in CSV files; the report
displays spaces instead. No source records were deleted due to quality flags.
